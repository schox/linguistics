# Doc

A utility or meta document: README, CONVENTIONS, AGENTS, DECISIONS, and the tool shims (CLAUDE.md, GEMINI.md).

`status`: `living` (kept current) | `frozen` (historical, do not edit).

Docs do not need `belongs_to`, `subfield` or `cites`.

## Body

No skeleton, and `Doc` is **exempt from the drafting policy** in `CONVENTIONS.md`. These are working documents rather than reference entries: they are allowed to argue, to address the reader, to carry judgement, and to be organized however the document needs. `ROADMAP.md` says so of itself at the top.

They are not exempt from house style, which is mechanical and applies everywhere.
