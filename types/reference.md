# Reference

A bibliographic entry: one note per source, cited from many. Filename and title use a citation key of author and year (`barber-1974.md`, titled `Barber 1974`).

Ref type: paper | book | chapter | website | dataset | course | thesis | corpus.
Status is the reading list: unread | in-progress | read. Do not mark something read that you have skimmed.
Prefer a DOI or stable publisher URL. Where an open-access copy exists alongside the version of record, give both.
