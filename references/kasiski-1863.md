# Kasiski 1863

The first published general method for breaking periodic polyalphabetic ciphers, ending the Vigenère cipher's three-century reputation as *le chiffre indéchiffrable*.

Charles Babbage had broken a variant by 1854 but did not publish, so priority and credit diverge here, which is a recurring pattern worth noting rather than a curiosity.
