# Torres Torres 2007

An account of the cuneiform decipherment written for its 150th anniversary, in Spanish, open access through the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid repository. Read for this vault.

It is the source for nearly everything in [Cuneiform](../Decipherment/cuneiform.md), and it is unusually good for this vault's purposes because it treats the **1857 verification test** as the centrepiece rather than as an anecdote. Most accounts make the decipherment end with Rawlinson at [Behistun](../places/behistun.md); this one makes it end with four men translating the same text in separate rooms.

Its English abstract states the shape of the argument: the decipherment "was officially accepted by the Royal Asiatic Society on May 29, 1857", the method "encountered general academic skepticism", and to dispel it the Society ran a test in which Hincks, Rawlinson, Oppert and Talbot each independently translated an inscription found at Qalat Shergat, the Tiglath-Pileser I prism.

Also useful for the long prehistory: Pietro della Valle noticing the script at Persepolis in the seventeenth century, Chardin's better copy in 1674, Niebuhr's accurate copies in 1788, Duperron's 1771 publication of the Zend Avesta which supplied the Avestan and Pahlavi comparanda, and Grotefend's 1802 work on the Persepolis royal inscriptions.

The footnotes carry substantial biographies of Hincks, Rawlinson, Talbot and Oppert, which is where this vault's `Person` notes get their detail.

**On quoting it.** The article is in Spanish. Passages rendered in English in the vault's notes are translations made here and are marked as such, with the Spanish given where the wording carries weight. The English abstract is quoted directly and contains one evident typo, "a long journey viaje", where the Spanish word was left in.
