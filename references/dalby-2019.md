# Dalby 2019

A peer-reviewed review article on the Rosetta Stone, received 17 June 2018 and accepted 20 February 2019, published open access under CC BY. Read in full for this vault's purposes.

The WikiJournal model is unusual and worth naming: it is a peer-reviewed journal whose articles are also released into Wikipedia, so the text will look familiar to anyone who has read the encyclopedia entry. That does not make it a tertiary source. It carries a DOI, named authorship, a review process and a publication record, which is what the vault's rule about encyclopedias is actually testing for.

Its value here is that it dates the decipherment step by step rather than compressing it into a moment, which is precisely what [Egyptian hieroglyphs](../Decipherment/egyptian-hieroglyphs.md) needs in order to say what actually broke the script.

It is also careful about the credit dispute between Young and Champollion, giving the specific contributions, the specific accusations, and who made them.
