# UNESCO, Archaeological Ruins at Moenjodaro

The World Heritage documentation for the site, inscribed in 1980 under criterion (iii) as bearing "exceptional testimony to the Indus civilization".

Cited here for one figure that is load-bearing in [Mohenjo-daro](../places/mohenjo-daro.md): the property comprises burnt brick structures "covering 240 ha, of which only about one third has been excavated since 1922". The argument that the absence of long Indus texts is evidence rather than accident depends on how thoroughly the sites have been dug, and this is the only quantification of that the vault has found.

Also gives the location (right bank of the Indus, 510 km north-east of Karachi, 28 km from Larkana) and records that the foundations are threatened by salinity from a rising water table, the subject of a UNESCO campaign in the 1970s.

**A dating caveat.** This page puts the Indus civilization at 2500-1500 BC, which is looser and later at the tail than the c. 2600-1900 BCE that [Farmer, Sproat and Witzel 2004](farmer-sproat-witzel-2004.md) and UNESCO's own [Harappa documentation](unesco-harappa-tentative.md) both give for the mature phase. Prefer the phase chronology in the Harappa entry, which is explicitly the excavators'.
