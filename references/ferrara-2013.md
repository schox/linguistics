# Ferrara 2013

The current corpus edition of the Cypro-Minoan inscriptions, and the successor to [Olivier 2007](olivier-2007.md). Volume I, *Analysis*, appeared in 2012; this is Volume II.

Figures used in [Cypro-Minoan](../Decipherment/cypro-minoan.md), taken from Vassilis Petrakis's review rather than from the book: 243 artifacts in the main catalog, numbered ##001-243, of which 27 are additions not present in Olivier's *HoChyMin*, plus a further addendum item. Appendix 2 tabulates sign variants as "83 different graphemes of a single writing system".

That last phrase is the argument, not just a count. The conventional classification splits the corpus into CM1, CM2 and CM3 (with CM0 for the earliest Enkomi tablet), following Masson and retained by Olivier. Ferrara's treatment of the repertory as variants of one system cuts against reading those as three scripts. The distinction matters directly for decipherability: three separate corpora of seventy-odd inscriptions each are further below any workable threshold than one corpus of 243.

**Unread.** The counts and the single-system claim come from the review, which is an independent scholarly summary but not the book. The publisher URL was not verified as resolving.
