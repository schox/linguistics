# UNESCO, Archaeological Site of Harappa

The tentative-list submission for Harappa, which is not an inscribed World Heritage property. More useful than the Moenjodaro entry despite that, because it reports the excavators' own stratigraphy rather than a summary.

The phase chronology it gives is the most precise the vault holds for the Indus material, and it is stated as the excavators' proposal: Ravi aspect of the Hakra phase c. 3300-2800 BC; Kot Dijian (Early Harappan) c. 2800-2600 BC; Harappan c. 2600-1900 BC; Transitional c. 1900-1800 BC; Late Harappan c. 1800-1300 BC. Its mature-phase dates agree with [Farmer, Sproat and Witzel 2004](farmer-sproat-witzel-2004.md), which is worth noting given that the two are on opposite sides of nothing but happen to be independent.

Also gives the site's full extent as 150 hectares, of which the property and buffer zone cover eight mounds and two cemeteries with the remainder under farmland and the modern village; an archaeological sequence over 13 metres deep; and an extent of over 100 hectares reached during the mature Harappan period.

**`year` is a proxy.** The page carries no publication or revision date. The year recorded is the year of access, and it establishes nothing about when the submission was written. Tentative-list entries are periodically revised, so a future reader should re-check rather than assume this text is stable.
