# Glottolog

The reference catalog of the world's languages, dialects and families, with a bibliography of descriptive works (over 460,000 references). Run out of the Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology as part of the CLLD ecosystem.

Its central contribution for this vault is the **Glottocode**: a unique, stable identifier for every languoid, meaning family, language or dialect alike. That is the join key between a `Language` note here and the external data, and the reason genealogy works as this vault's canonical spine: Glottolog already maintains the tree, so the vault does not have to.

Versioned and released with a DOI per version, so cite the version used. CC BY 4.0, which means data may be reused with attribution.
