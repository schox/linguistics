# Salgarella 2025

A short single-author overview of Linear A by the creator of [SigLA](salgarella-castellan-2020.md), written at the Aarhus Institute of Advanced Studies. First published online August 2025. Hardback 9781009520027, paperback 9781009520065, series ISSN 2753-6378 online and 2753-636X print.

This is the reference that would close the largest number of open questions in [Linear A](../Decipherment/linear-a.md) at once, and its ten sections map almost exactly onto them:

1. Defining the (un)definable: what is Linear A?
2. A tale of life and death: what is the lifespan of Linear A?
3. The Linear A corpus: where is Linear A found?
4. Drawing lines: what does Linear A look like?
5. Of clay and stone (and else): where does Linear A appear?
6. (Beyond) accounting: what was Linear A used for?
7. 'Elementary, my dear Watson': what do we know from reading Linear A?
8. Speaking in riddles: which language does Linear A encode?
9. More unresolved mysteries: what do we not have in Linear A?
10. Current and future pathways of research: what's next?

Sections 3 and 5 would settle the per-site distribution of the 1,427 documents, which is the standing gap on both [Linear A](../Decipherment/linear-a.md) and [Hagia Triada](../places/hagia-triada.md). Section 2 bears on the dating disagreement between Salgarella and Castellan, Rutter, and Petrolito and colleagues. Section 8 covers the candidate languages.

The abstract gives the date range as ca. 1800-1450 BCE, matching what the vault already records, and states that Linear A "is still undeciphered, and the language it encodes ('Minoan') thus remains elusive".
