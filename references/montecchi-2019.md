# Montecchi 2019

Reported to be the first comprehensive study of the Linear A archive from [Hagia Triada](../places/hagia-triada.md), which is the largest such archive known: the tablets, the sealed documents, and the economic and administrative system they record, in Late Minoan IB.

This is the work of record for any statement about the size and composition of that archive, which is why it has a note despite being unread.

**Not consulted, and the counts here are unverified.** Repository records for the volume were unreachable from this environment: the CNR eprints server failed on a TLS error and the Bologna repository returned 403. What could be established from independent catalog records is the author, title, year, publisher and series, all of which agree across the Arbor Sapientiae, Bologna and CNR listings. Two different ISBNs appeared in those listings, so none is recorded here.

The figure that circulates in tertiary sources for this archive, 147 tablets and 22 roundels alongside a much larger body of noduli and nodules, could not be traced to this volume or to any source that was actually read. It is therefore not stated as fact in [Hagia Triada](../places/hagia-triada.md). The one tablet count this vault does assert comes from [Rutter](rutter-aegean-prehistory.md), who gives a different number. Reading this volume would settle it.
