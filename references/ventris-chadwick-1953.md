# Ventris and Chadwick 1953

The formal publication of the Linear B decipherment, following Ventris's BBC announcement of 1 July 1952. The paper is the scholarly case rather than the announcement, and Chadwick's involvement is what made it one.

Worth reading for what counts as evidence in a decipherment claim: not that a reading can be produced, but that it predicts correctly on text it was not built from.
