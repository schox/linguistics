# Farmer, Sproat and Witzel 2004

Argues that the Indus corpus is not writing at all: that the sign sequences are too short, the sign inventory behaves wrongly, and the absence of long texts is not an accident of preservation but evidence of a non-linguistic symbol system.

The strongest available statement of the position that a decipherment problem may be ill-posed, and the necessary counterweight to any assumption that an undeciphered corpus must encode language.
