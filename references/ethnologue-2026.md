# Ethnologue 2026

The standard catalog of the world's languages and the usual source for speaker numbers. Held here as the popularity half of the language selection, and used only as far as its free pages actually go.

## What it establishes

**The ranking exists and is authoritative**, published as "The Ethnologue 200" and, in the publisher's words, "often cited by top-tier publications". The list "accounts for most of the world's population", with over 88% of people using one of its languages natively.

**The top four, which are stated in prose and therefore readable without the data:**

- By **first-language** speakers: Mandarin Chinese, then Spanish, then English, then Hindi.
- By **total** speakers, counting second and later languages: English, then Mandarin Chinese, then Hindi, then Spanish.

English is recorded as spoken in 188 countries against Mandarin's 108, which is the reason the two orderings differ.

**A methodological caveat that changes what the ranking means**, in the publisher's words: the list "ranks individual languages, not macrolanguages". So Chinese and Arabic do not appear as entries; Mandarin does, and "if macrolanguages were included, entries like Chinese and Arabic would each encompass multiple distinct languages, changing the structure of the list considerably". Mandarin is described as the largest of nineteen languages grouped as Chinese by a shared writing system and literature.

That caveat is why the selection built on this names Mandarin Chinese and Modern Standard Arabic rather than "Chinese" and "Arabic".

## Access

**The ranked list itself is not freely available.** The 2026 list is served through a search widget, with the full list sold as a download. The pages consulted give the top four in prose and nothing further.

Both pages were retrieved through the Tavily extraction service after `ethnologue.com` returned HTTP 403 to a direct fetch, so the content above was not seen in a browser. That is the fallback `AGENTS.md` permits, and it is recorded here because the facts above are load-bearing for the selection.

**Consequence for this corpus, stated so it is not mistaken for a full application of the source:** only the four languages named above are selected on verified Ethnologue evidence. Every other language in the selection is there for genealogical coverage, chosen from this area's own index, and is marked as such on the hub. Purchasing the list, or reaching the twenty-ninth edition through an institution, would let the popularity half be done properly.
