# Kelley 2026

A short volume on Proto-Elamite in the Cambridge Elements series *Writing in the Ancient World*, published online 18 July 2026 and in print on 6 August 2026, by Kathryn Kelley of Uppsala University. Online ISBN 9781009614559.

The current statement of the field, and the source for the figures in [Proto-Elamite](../Decipherment/proto-elamite.md): approximately 1,700 clay tablets from Iran dating to roughly 3300-2900 BCE, from eight or nine excavation sites, using several hundred non-numerical signs. At least 620 are in Iran's National Museum and over a thousand are in the Louvre and other collections.

Its framing of the decipherment status is careful and worth keeping: the script is "only very partly deciphered", with the numerical systems substantially understood and the non-numerical signs read as encoding commodities, personnel and institutions ideographically rather than as a primarily linguistic encoding. That is a different kind of partial success from the Aegean cases and the note should not flatten it.

The abstract also notes that Proto-Elamite scholarship has been marginal relative to its importance, and that recent progress has come from corpus digitization and computational methods.
