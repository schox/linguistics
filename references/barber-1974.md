# Barber 1974

Elizabeth Wayland Barber's handbook of decipherment method, and the standard statement of the **evidentiary threshold**: the quantity of text below which a proposed decipherment can be neither proved nor disproved.

Barber has since restated the position directly in relation to Linear A, noting that the corpus has not grown enough since 1974 to cross the threshold if the underlying language is unknown or structurally unusual, and that this limit is not removed by better algorithms.
