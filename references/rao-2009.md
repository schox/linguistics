# Rao et al. 2009

Argues from conditional entropy that Indus sign sequences pattern like linguistic systems rather than like non-linguistic sign systems, and therefore that the corpus plausibly encodes language.

Contested. See [Farmer, Sproat and Witzel 2004](farmer-sproat-witzel-2004.md) for the opposing position, which preceded it, and Rao's published rebuttal for the reply.
