# Assael et al. 2022 (Ithaca)

Ithaca restores missing text in damaged Greek inscriptions and attributes them by date and place. Its successor Aeneas (2025) does comparable work for Latin.

Filed here as the standing example of the category error this vault tries to avoid: **restoration is not decipherment**. Ithaca operates on a script and language already fully understood. Popular coverage routinely presents it as AI reading lost languages, which it is not.
