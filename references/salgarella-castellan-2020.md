# Salgarella and Castellan 2020

An open-access database of Linear A inscriptions with hand-drawn sign variants, built because the corpus of record ([GORILA](godart-olivier-1976.md)) exists only in print and so cannot be analyzed statistically or paleographically without re-collecting it by hand. Salgarella was at St John's College, Cambridge; Castellan at Inria, Univ Rennes, CNRS, IRISA.

The accompanying paper is the source for the sign inventory used in [Linear A](../Decipherment/linear-a.md): "Linear A is composed of some 180 simple signs (representing a graphic and phonologic unit); and some 164 complex (or composite) signs (which are the combination of two or more simple signs), on top of these there are some 30 fractional signs", taken from the standard sign list in GORILA volume 5, pp. xxii-xxvii.

It is also the source for the date range ca. 1800-1450 BCE, for the description of Linear A as logo-syllabic, and for the statement that Minoan "does not seem to be related to any of the Indo-European languages so far known (most notably Greek), nor does it to Semitic ones".

The drawings and dataset are stated to be available under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. That is a share-alike, non-commercial license, so it is a link-out rather than a mirror under the vault's stance in `_data-sources.md`.

**Bibliographic gap.** The paper carries the date 20 August 2020 and the authors' affiliations, but the copy reached here states no journal, conference or publisher. A related record exists in the Cambridge repository and a copy is hosted by Fluxus Editions, which suggests a conference volume, but the venue was not established. Cite it as a database with its accompanying paper rather than as a journal article until that is settled.
