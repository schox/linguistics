# WALS Online

A database of structural properties of languages, phonological, grammatical and lexical, compiled by 55 researchers from reference grammars, and mappable geographically. Published as a book with CD-ROM in 2005 and online since 2008.

The model worth studying here is that each feature (basic word order, presence of tone, alignment type) is a curated, citable chapter with an author, and the data layer sits under it. Typological features become queryable and comparable across languages rather than buried in prose.

**WALS is complete and no longer updated.** That makes it stable to cite and safe to mirror, unlike Glottolog which moves. Cite individual chapters rather than the work as a whole unless drawing on 25 or more.
