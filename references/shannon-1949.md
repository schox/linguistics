# Shannon 1949

The founding paper of theoretical cryptography. Introduces perfect secrecy, redundancy, equivocation, and **unicity distance**: the minimum quantity of ciphertext at which a unique solution exists. Below it the problem is underdetermined and multiple keys yield plausible plaintexts.

The unicity result is the reason this note matters outside cryptography. It is the same argument, reached independently, as the evidentiary threshold in archaeological decipherment.
