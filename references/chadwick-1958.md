# Chadwick 1958

The standard first-hand account of the Linear B decipherment, written by Ventris's collaborator two years after Ventris's death.

Valuable as method rather than only as history: it sets out what the decipherment actually turned on, including [Kober](../people/alice-kober.md)'s structural groundwork, Ventris's grid, and the moment the Greek reading became undeniable. Chadwick's assessment of Kober's contribution is one of the few contemporary judgements to give it proper weight.

Note the author's own interest: he is describing a decipherment he helped establish, so read the account of its reception with that in mind.
