# McCarthy 1960

The founding paper of Lisp, setting out s-expressions and the evaluator. Notable here as a case of a language specified as a **notation** first and implemented afterwards, which is the opposite of how most languages arrive and is the reason Lisp is the useful test case for comparing programming languages with human ones.
