# Hardness assumptions

**Stub.** Expected to cover the computational problems public-key cryptography rests on, and the fact that none is proved hard: security is conditional on assumptions that could fail. Distinct from the attack algorithms filed under [Cryptanalysis](cryptanalysis.md), which are what makes the assumptions measurable.
