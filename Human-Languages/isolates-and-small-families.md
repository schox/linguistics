# Isolates and small families

Languages with no relatives, or almost none: Basque, Sumerian, Elamite, the Tyrsenian family, Ainu, Burushaski. A junction with Decipherment, because an isolate offers no comparative leverage, which is precisely what makes a script recording one hard to read.

**Two of the four are not single languages.** Glottolog treats both Ainu and Nivkh as small families rather than isolates, with three and two child languages respectively. The notes record the split rather than flattening it, and each says which languoid its identifiers point at. That is the same distinction [Etruscan](etruscan.md) turns on, where a language long filed as an isolate is now placed in the Tyrsenian family, and it decides how much comparative leverage a decipherer has.
