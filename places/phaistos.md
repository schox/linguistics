# Phaistos

A Minoan palace site in south-central Crete, and the provenance of exactly one inscribed object that matters to this vault.

Pleiades describes it as an ancient settlement where occupation began around 3000 BCE, a major center of the Minoan civilization that continued as a Greek city into the first millennium BCE. It lies a few kilometers from [Hagia Triada](hagia-triada.md), with which it is usually discussed as a pair.

## Why the site matters here

Luigi Pernier found the [Phaistos Disc](../Decipherment/phaistos-disc.md) in a room of the palace on 3 July 1908.

The provenance argument here runs the opposite way to every other site in the vault. [Knossos](knossos.md) mattered because it yielded thousands of tablets; [Hagia Triada](hagia-triada.md) because it yielded the largest Linear A archive; [Mohenjo-daro](mohenjo-daro.md) because of what a heavily excavated site failed to produce. Phaistos matters because a well-excavated Minoan palace produced **one** object in this script and nothing else resembling it, anywhere, in the following century.

That is what makes the Disc undecipherable rather than merely undeciphered, and it is a fact about excavation rather than about method. A second disc would change the problem completely.

Phaistos has also produced Linear A material, which is why it appears in the findspot lists in [Rutter](../references/rutter-aegean-prehistory.md) alongside Knossos, Mallia, Archanes and Zakro. The vault does not have per-site counts for those, so `found_at` on the Linear A note does not currently list it.

## Open questions

- **The excavation history is not recorded here.** Pernier's own reports, announced in *Ausonia* in 1908 with a fuller account in 1909, are the primary record for the Disc's findspot and are what would settle the disturbed-context problem flagged in the script note. Neither has been read, and the Italian mission's wider work at Phaistos is not covered.
- **The site's Linear A material is unquantified**, as for every Linear A findspot other than Hagia Triada. See the open question in [Linear A](../Decipherment/linear-a.md).
