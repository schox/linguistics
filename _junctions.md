# Junctions

The notes that join areas. These carry the vault's actual argument, which is that linguistics, cryptography and decipherment share an apparatus, and that the sharing is precise rather than decorative.

Folders cannot express this, since each note lives in one folder but belongs to several subjects. This index and the `related_to` graph do the work instead.

## Cryptography and Decipherment

- [Why a script stays unread](Decipherment/why-scripts-stay-unread.md). The synthesis the whole Decipherment area was building toward: two families of obstacle, one evidential and computable in advance, one not, and only the first justifies calling a script undecipherable.
- [The evidentiary threshold and unicity distance](Decipherment/evidentiary-threshold-and-unicity-distance.md). Shannon and Barber reached the same underdetermination result independently. The flagship link.
- [Adversarial versus accidental encipherment](Decipherment/adversarial-versus-accidental.md). Why the statistical apparatus transfers and the adversarial apparatus does not.
- [Frequency analysis](Decipherment/frequency-analysis.md). One technique, three literatures, from al-Kindi to Kober.
- [Is it writing at all? The Indus entropy debate](Decipherment/is-it-writing-at-all.md). Entropy and information content as a test of well-posedness, and now the argument that well-posedness and corpus size are two terms of one expression rather than two problems.

## Computation and Decipherment

- [Computational decipherment: three distinct tasks](Decipherment/computational-decipherment-three-tasks.md). Restoration, decipherment proper, and cognate search, routinely conflated.
