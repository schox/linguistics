# Practitioners

The practice of decipherment as carried by its people: philologists who learned cryptanalysis, and cryptanalysts who crossed over. `Person` notes themselves live in `people/`; this hub is for notes about practice, careers and the sociology of the field.

**Stub hub.** This note exists so the taxonomy is visible in the graph before the subfield has content; the breadth program in `ROADMAP.md` fills it in. Notes in this subfield set `belongs_to` here once it has an owner below the area hub.

All eleven are named as owed in `ROADMAP.md` or in the area index, so no selection principle was needed: the corpus already refers to each of them. They live in `people/`.

- [Elizabeth Wayland Barber](../people/barber.md), whose evidentiary threshold this area's central claim rests on
- [Horapollo](../people/horapollo.md) and [Athanasius Kircher](../people/kircher.md), the corpus's first notes for people who **blocked** a decipherment rather than achieving one
- [Diego de Landa](../people/diego-de-landa.md)
- [J. Eric S. Thompson](../people/thompson.md), currently characterized here only through his opponents, which `ROADMAP.md` calls the least defensible thing in this area
- [Claude Schaeffer](../people/schaeffer.md), who bridges Ugarit and Enkomi
- [Thomas Barthel](../people/barthel.md), for the rongorongo corpus
- [Hans Bauer](../people/bauer.md), [Edouard Dhorme](../people/dhorme.md) and [Charles Virolleaud](../people/virolleaud.md), the three who read Ugaritic
- [al-Kindi](../people/al-kindi.md), the earliest written account of frequency analysis
